RNS-E 192 SD bridge, revision A
Dimensions in mm. Single insert per Gerber outline. Bare PCB 21.50 x 30.60.
The separate insulating carrier provides the SD outline and latch.

Finished four-layer FR-4 0.40 +/-0.04 including mask and finish. Keep flat.
External copper target 18 um finished, inner copper 12 um. Nominal dielectric
spacings 0.075 / 0.170 / 0.075. L1 components/signals, L2 continuous ground,
L3 signals and 3.3 V pour, L4 fingers and signals. 0.15 min track, 0.13 min
clearance, 0.20 finished PTH via in 0.45 land. Tent vias both sides.
Controlled Z target for L1 USB full-speed pair: 90 ohm differential where
coupled. Fabricator to review the stack against the KiCad geometry before
production. The SD contact fanout necessarily separates the pair.

Selective electroplated hard gold 0.76 um minimum over 2.5 um minimum nickel
on all nine B.Cu contact lands, including electrically unused J1.1. B.Mask
defines all nine wipe surfaces. ENIG for F.Cu assembly lands. Supply the fab
with these notes and B.Mask when quoting the internal, isolated gold lands.
The fabricator must remove any temporary plating connections in CAM/panel
processing. Verify all nine nets against the source board after depaneling.
Keep paste, solder, lettering, burrs and exposed via holes off the wipe areas.
Route the outline to +/-0.05 and remove tabs flush. Deburr the insertion edge.
Panel support is needed for reflow of this thin board. Assembly is top only.
Use the listed MPNs and confirm pin 1 on U1, U2 and Y1 and K on D1 against
the KiCad footprints. No physical parts at J1 or TP1-7. No paste on contacts.
